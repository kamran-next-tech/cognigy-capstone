Questions

Where do I find my order number?

In your confirmation email. It starts with ORD followed by six digits, for example ORD-100003.

Can I cancel after my order has shipped?

No. Orders can be cancelled only while they are processing. Once it arrives, you can start a return instead.

Ken read me a PIN. What do I do?

For changes, returns and missing-item reports you can fill in a short form instead of spelling details out. Click Enter PIN in the call window and type the six characters Ken reads out. Stay on the call; Ken continues as soon as you submit the form. You can also just tell Ken the details.

Can I speak German?

Yes. Just start speaking German and Ken will switch.

Can I talk to a person?

Yes. Ask Ken at any time and your call will be transferred to our support team.
